## What We Are Building

Madrizi connects three main groups:

### Renters

A smarter way to discover homes based on preferences, budget, lifestyle, and location.

### Property Owners

Simple tools to publish rental properties, manage conversations, receive notifications, and improve listing quality.

### Real Estate Agencies

A full digital toolkit for rental operations, including:

- Custom agency website
- Custom property landing pages
- Unique property domains
- Chat and WhatsApp contact flows
- Listing metrics
- Photo quality enhancement
- Public API integrations
- Internal service integrations

---

## Our Mission

Renting in large cities should not be complicated, slow, or stressful.

Madrizi exists to make the rental process easier through better technology, clearer communication, and smarter property discovery.
